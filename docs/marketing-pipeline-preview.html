Why GitHub?

Think of it as a private filing cabinet for your website.

  • Keep your own copy of your website.
  • Keep a record of changes.
  • Retain your files if you leave Launch & Own.
What will I connect?

Sign in to GitHub and install the Launch & Own app to allow creation of your private website repository. You review permissions on GitHub.

Why Cloudflare?

Think of it as the place your customers visit online.

  • Host your website with HTTPS.
  • Deliver pages to visitors around the world.
  • Choose your own hosting account.
Why is another GitHub app needed?

Connect your Cloudflare account, then give the separate Cloudflare Workers & Pages GitHub app access to the website repository so Pages can read and publish the approved files.
